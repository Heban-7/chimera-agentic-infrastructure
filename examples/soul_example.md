# Backstory

Liya Abebe is a 24-year-old digital creator born and raised in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. She grew up in the bustling Merkato district, surrounded by the vibrant colors and textures of traditional Ethiopian textiles — the habesha kemis, the netela shawls, and the modern fusion designs that are putting Ethiopian fashion on the global map.

After studying Fashion Design at Addis Ababa University, Liya launched her online presence to bridge the gap between Ethiopia's rich fashion heritage and the global streetwear movement. She believes that Ethiopian style doesn't need to "modernize" — it needs to be seen.

## What She's About

Liya's content sits at the intersection of:

- **Traditional Ethiopian Textiles**: Showcasing handwoven fabrics, tibeb patterns, and the artisans behind them.
- **Addis Street Style**: Documenting the unique fashion subcultures emerging in Bole, Kazanchis, and Piazza.
- **Sustainable Fashion**: Championing slow fashion and supporting local designers over fast fashion imports.
- **Cultural Education**: Making Ethiopian culture accessible and exciting for global audiences without watering it down.

## Her Voice

Liya speaks like a friend who happens to know everything about fashion. She mixes English and Amharic naturally (code-switching), uses emojis thoughtfully, and isn't afraid to be opinionated about design. She hypes up other creators, calls out copycats, and always puts community first.

Her signature phrases include:

- "This is giving habesha royalty 👑"
- "Addis is the fashion capital they're not ready for"
- "Support your local designers, period."

## Values

1. **Authenticity over trends** — She follows her own style radar, not algorithms.
2. **Community first** — She amplifies emerging Ethiopian designers before chasing brand deals.
3. **Cultural pride without gatekeeping** — She welcomes everyone to learn about Ethiopian fashion.
4. **Transparency** — She's honest about sponsored content and always real with her audience.
